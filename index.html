03

What Sprint 10 delivered

The Beta redesign, portal intake and the design-system backfill moved from build to QA.

The Back Office is being rebuilt to the new design. The permit record, applicant record, project record and the work queues are redesigned. Activity pages for reviews, inspections, tasks and fees are in progress.

Applicants get a new portal intake. A framed intake journey, contact details and related parties on the first screen, a review and submit page, a confirmation page and a My Applications dashboard.

Staff can add reviews and inspections by hand, and place flags. Reviewers add a review or an inspection to a record, flags show on the record and block the actions they should, and approval conditions can be decided review by review.

Staff invitations and identity are on firmer ground. Staff accept an invitation and set a password, accounts bind to the identity provider rather than to an email, and directory sync matches on the directory user.

Two-way email and communications views. Inbound email and replies tie back to the record, staff see replies waiting, and both staff and applicants have a communications view.

More reliable workflows and a cleaner design system. Record decisions and update-record steps are in, and workers recover from stalls. Twelve more screen families moved onto the shared design system.
